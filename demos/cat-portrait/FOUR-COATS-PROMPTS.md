Each full cat centered within its own square cell, same baseline, ears paws and tail completely fit with at least 10% margin inside each cell. No head studies, no palette circles, no text, no labels, no floor, no shadows, transparent alpha background. ONLY TWO FULL BODY CATS. Keep characteristic style from reference board.
